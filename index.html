🌿 What is ChronoLeaf?

ChronoLeaf is a cheerful countdown and event tracking app built specifically for Windows. It’s designed for ordinary people — no tech skills needed. You open it, add your important dates, and instantly see how many days (hours, minutes, and seconds!) remain until that special moment.

Think of it as a dedicated digital calendar companion for the most exciting days in your life: birthdays of loved ones, anniversaries, big trips, product launches, exam days, or even your next payday. ChronoLeaf shows every countdown in a calm, tidy list with clear visual highlights.

📖 How to Use ChronoLeaf (For Everyone)

You don't need a manual to use this app, but here's a quick tour to make you instantly confident:

➕ Adding an Event

Click the "+" (plus) button in the top-left corner. Type a name (e.g. "Birthday! 🎂"), pick the date, and optionally set a time. Click save. Done.

👀 Watching Countdowns

Your events appear as colorful cards. Each card shows the event name and the exact time remaining — updating every second.

🔕 Changing Notifications

Right-click an event to choose how far in advance you want a reminder (like one day before or one hour). Turn off anytime.

That's all there is to it. The app is deliberately simple so you can set it up and forget it — until your happy day arrives.

  • RAM: At least 2 GB (4 GB recommended for smooth multitasking).

  • Internet: Only needed for downloading; after that, everything runs offline.
